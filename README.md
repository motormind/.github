# MotorMind

**We measure how car software feels to the people who actually use it.**

Modern cars pass every regulatory test and still frustrate their drivers: climate
controls buried three menus deep, voice assistants that give up at motorway speed,
phone connections that drop mid-route. MotorMind turns that frustration into data
that is structured, comparable and traceable to its cause.

## What we work on

- **Expert testing** – multimodal in-car test sessions (video, audio, vehicle and sensor data), coded by trained reviewers
- **Driver feedback** – continuous signals from real owners across a vehicle's whole life, not one survey after purchase
- **AI that scales it** – models trained on expert judgements to read large volumes of user feedback
- **Research-grade methods** – documented protocols and reproducible analysis, so results can be cited and checked
